Programs

STEM Bridge Scholarship

Full scholarships for public and community school graduates entering STEM programs. We cover tuition balances, hostels, lab fees, and a rugged laptop.

  • Eligibility: enrolled at Tribhuvan, KU, or CTEVT campus
  • Priority: first-generation students with financial need
  • Support: mentoring, emergency stipends, quarterly check-ins

Future Coders Fellowship

Weekend labs in Janakpur, Pokhara, and Surkhet using Scratch, Python, and GIS to solve local problems with Nepali mentors.

  • Eligibility: Grades 8–12 with interest in coding and design
  • Support: tablets/laptops, routers, solar backup, travel meals
  • Outcome: publish first app or project before SEE exams

Get support

Students, teachers, ward partners: start with an email.

There’s no online form. Email us with your school contact and what support is needed. We verify with headteachers and coordinate next steps.

  • Email info@aashirbadfoundation.org
  • Share your school, ward/municipality, and what’s needed
  • We schedule a call with headteachers to confirm
