Scholarship pathways

Programs that meet scholars where they are.

Each initiative mixes financial aid, mentoring, and digital access so students can move from secondary school to college to career without interruption.

Programs

STEM Scholarship Track

Full scholarships keep university students in computer science, engineering, and data science on track by covering tuition, housing, lab fees, and a laptop stipend.

  • Career coaching and internship placements
  • Emergency microgrants for health or housing
  • Peer cohorts for accountability

Impact: 110 scholars have graduated with STEM degrees since 2018.

Future Coders Fellowship

High school students join weekend coding labs, receive laptops, and collaborate with mentors who work in Nepal's growing technology sector.

  • Monthly hack days hosted with partner schools
  • Hardware library with tablets and robotics kits
  • Stipends for transportation and meals

Impact: 75% of fellows build their first apps or websites before graduation.

Bridge to University Fund

Bridge grants help rural students pay for entrance exams, travel to Kathmandu, safe housing, and other living costs that appear during their first year.

  • Travel escorts and safe dormitory partners
  • Transition counseling for families
  • Small business grants for caregivers

Impact: 60 students relocated safely and completed their first year with support.

Women in Tech Leadership Labs

Weekend leadership intensives, mentorship circles, and small seed funds help women scholars launch tech for good ventures in their hometowns.

  • Pitch coaching and impact storytelling
  • Connections to angel mentors and accelerators
  • Mini-grants for prototypes serving local needs

Impact: 30 alumni have launched community technology initiatives.

Digital Access Fund

Provides tablets, reliable connectivity stipends, and localized curriculum so students in remote villages can learn online.

  • Community Wi-Fi hubs and solar charging kits
  • Tech support hotline staffed by volunteers
  • Localized Nepali-English STEM curriculum

Impact: 1,200+ devices distributed across 25 districts.
