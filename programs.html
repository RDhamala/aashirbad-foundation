Programs

STEM Bridge Scholarship

Full scholarships keep students from public and community schools on track at Tribhuvan, Kathmandu University, and CTEVT campuses by covering tuition balances, hostels, lab fees, and a rugged laptop.

  • Career coaching with alumni at Deerwalk, Fusemachines, Khalti, and Vianet
  • Emergency stipends for healthcare, visas, or family crises
  • Peer accountability circles facilitated by Teach For Nepal alumni

Impact: 110 scholars have earned STEM degrees since 2018 with 78% staying in Nepal’s tech sector.

Future Coders Fellowship

Weekend labs in Janakpur, Pokhara, and Surkhet blend Scratch, Python, GIS, and climate data so students can solve hyperlocal problems with mentors from Karkhana Samuha and Girls in Tech Nepal.

  • Monthly hack days co-hosted with public schools and municipal ICT clubs
  • Hardware library with tablets, routers, and solar chargers
  • Travel meals and SIM top-ups for students commuting from villages

Impact: 75% of fellows publish their first apps or dashboards before SEE exams.

Get support

Students, teachers, ward partners: start with an email.

There’s no online form. Email us with your school contact and what support is needed. We verify with headteachers and coordinate next steps.

  • Email info@aashirbadfoundation.org
  • Share your school, ward/municipality, and what’s needed
  • We schedule a call with headteachers to confirm
