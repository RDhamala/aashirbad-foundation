Our Work

Education support, mentorship, and digital opportunity.

We work with public schools, ward committees, and alumni mentors in Dhading, Nepal to help students stay in school, connect with guidance, and gain meaningful exposure to technology and future opportunities.

Programs

Education Support

We help students overcome financial barriers that can interrupt their education, such as school fees, exam fees, and books. Headteachers verify each recipient, and donors receive updates every term.

  • Needs-based support verified by headteachers and ward committees
  • Covers tuition, hostel, lab fees, books, and exam costs
  • Progress updates shared with donors each term

Impact: 100+ students supported since 2023 with 95% year-over-year retention.

Mentorship & Exposure

Weekly calls and career conversations with Nepali mentors who understand the journey. Mentors help students see paths and possibilities beyond their immediate surroundings, and check in on attendance, grades, and goals.

  • Weekly check-ins on attendance, grades, and personal goals
  • Career conversations with Nepali professionals, including in tech
  • Peer study groups and accountability circles

Impact: 40+ mentors volunteering weekly to coach students across Dhading.

Digital Opportunity

Many students in rural communities encounter computers much later than their peers elsewhere. We work with partner schools to provide shared computer access and guided digital learning, so students gain real exposure to technology and the paths it can open, well before they leave school.

  • Shared computer labs at partner schools, with setup and maintenance support
  • Guided digital literacy sessions alongside regular coursework
  • Early exposure to coding and technology careers, including weekend sessions for interested students

Impact: Structured digital access supported at 10+ partner classrooms since 2023.

Get support

Students, teachers, ward partners: start with an email.

There’s no online form. Email us with your school contact and what support is needed. We verify with headteachers and coordinate next steps.

  • Email info@aashirbadfoundation.org
  • Share your school, ward/municipality, and what’s needed
  • We schedule a call with headteachers to confirm
