Stories

Voices from classrooms across Nepal.

Scholars, mentors, and families share how scholarships and digital access have changed their path.

Student stories

Sita · Future Coders fellow

Sita used her scholarship to complete a computer science degree and now mentors rural students in Dolakha.

"The tablet I received let me keep up with coursework when floods closed our school. Mentors helped me publish my first Android app."

Rabin · Volunteer mentor

Volunteer engineer Rabin hosts online design reviews so fellows graduate with portfolio-ready projects.

"I review capstone projects live from Seattle so students can present confidently to hiring managers."

Manju · Women in Tech Lab alum

Manju built a Nepali-language coding curriculum for girls in Chitwan and now operates a solar-powered lab.

"Seed funding helped me convert a community hall into a digital classroom that stays open during load-shedding."

Principal Rai · Partner school

Our Chitwan partner school integrates leadership labs into its science curriculum thanks to training and microgrants.

"Retention is up 30 percent because students see a path into technology careers."
