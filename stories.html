Bagmati · Scholar

Sita brings laptops to Dolakha.

After finishing her BSc.CSIT at Tribhuvan University, Sita returned to her public school in Dolakha to run weekend laptop clubs powered by solar-charged batteries.

“Teach For Nepal showed us that community teachers can lead the change. When the landslide cut power, our solar trunk kept everyone's coursework alive.”
  • First woman coder from her ward
  • 18 girls enrolled in the Dolakha laptop club
Province 2 · Fellowship

Bikash codes in Janakpur.

Bikash uses refurbished tablets and free fiber from Nepal Telecom to host Scratch and Python sessions for students whose families migrate seasonally for work.

“Karkhana Samuha helped me design tinkering lessons in Maithili. Now parents stay in the classroom because they see their kids building apps for cooperatives.”
  • 6 municipal schools sharing one hub
  • 32 project demos published on GitHub
Gandaki · Women in Tech

Manju powers a solar classroom.

Inspired by organisations like Women Leaders in Technology, Manju built a Nepali-language curriculum and a solar microgrid so girls in Bharatpur can code even during load-shedding.

“Room to Read helped our library years ago; this grant let us add laptops and LED lights so the lab never shuts down.”
  • 30 leadership labs launched since 2021
  • Hybrid design challenges with Siddhartha Bank mentors
Chitwan · Partner school

Principal Rai scales leadership labs.

Principal Rai worked with the Education Cluster, Teach For Nepal alumni, and our team to weave digital leadership labs into the Grade 9 science period.

“Retention is up 30 percent because students see pathways like Deerwalk, Fusemachines, and Khalti hiring Nepali talent.”
  • 5 districts piloting the model
  • Quarterly open classrooms for nearby schools
