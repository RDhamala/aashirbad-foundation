Why We Started

Why We Started

Aashirbad Foundation began in the hills of Jyamrung, where our founder grew up studying under candlelight and walking steep trails just to reach school. The foundation is named after his late father, a village teacher who believed education was the greatest gift a child could receive. Today, we honor that legacy by helping Nepali students access scholarships, mentorship, and the tools they need to learn and thrive.
