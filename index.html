Mission

We invest in students who are building the future of Nepal.

Through scholarships, leadership coaching, and technology stipends, students gain the resilience and tools needed to graduate. Each scholar works with mentors to develop a learning plan, track progress, and give back to their home communities.

“Our mission is to empower aspiring minds by providing transformative scholarships. We are dedicated to breaking financial barriers and fostering inclusivity, ensuring that passionate students, especially in Nepal, can pursue their educational aspirations in technology and related fields.”
  • Tuition, exam fees, housing, and technology grants
  • Mentoring from Nepali engineers and global volunteers
  • Digital literacy for students and their families

What drives us

Education is a proven path to economic stability in Nepal. When a student stays in school, siblings follow, local businesses grow, and communities gain tech-ready leaders.

Our story

From Jyamrung to global technology hubs.

Aashirbad Foundation began with the life journey of Roshan Dhamala, who grew up in the remote hillside village of Jyamrung, Dhading. Electricity arrived when he was in grade eight, classrooms had no computers, and mobile coverage barely existed. His early education depended on candlelight, determination, and long walks across the mountains.

  • First computer experience came only after moving to Kathmandu for higher studies.
  • “Aashirbad” honors his late father, a rural high school teacher who believed education was the greatest gift.
  • Roshan later earned BS in Computer Science and MS in Software Engineering at Texas Tech University and now serves as a Senior Data Engineer at Veeva Systems.

He never forgot the students still climbing the same steep trails. The foundation exists because talent is universal, opportunity is not. Every scholarship honors his father’s blessing and opens doors for students facing the barriers he once did.
