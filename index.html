What we do

Simple programs, clear impact

Scholarships, mentorship, and devices keep students enrolled and learning across Dhading.

Scholarships

Fees, books, hostel, and exams covered so students don’t pause school mid-year.

100+ scholarships funded; 95% retention.

Mentorship

Weekly calls and career prep with Nepali mentors who understand the journey.

40+ mentors volunteering weekly across Dhading.

Device Support

Laptops and tablets with onboarding so rural classrooms can study and code.

10+ devices placed in rural classrooms.

Why We Started

From Dhading’s hills to a model for access

Aashirbad Foundation began in the hills of Jyamrung, where our founder grew up studying under candlelight and walking steep trails just to reach school. The foundation is named after his late father, a village teacher who believed education was the greatest gift a child could receive. Today, we pilot in Dhading-covering fees, pairing mentors, and placing devices-to prove a low-cost model we can scale to other rural classrooms.
