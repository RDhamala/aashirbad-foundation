What we do

Three programs, one goal

Everything we do is designed to keep students enrolled and learning.

Scholarships

Tuition, books, hostel, and exams covered so students don’t have to pause school mid-year.

100+ scholarships funded since 2023

Mentorship

Weekly calls and career prep with 40+ Nepali mentors who understand the journey.

95% of scholars stay enrolled year over year

Device Support

Laptops and tablets placed in rural classrooms with setup and maintenance support.

10+ devices placed in Dhading schools
