Mission

We invest in students who are building the future of Nepal.

Scholarships, leadership coaching, tech stipends, and essentials such as winter supplies or travel grants give students the footing they need to stay in school. Each scholar works with mentors in Nepal and the diaspora to develop a learning plan, track progress, and return skills to their home communities.

“Our mission is to empower aspiring minds by providing transformative scholarships. We are dedicated to breaking financial barriers and fostering inclusivity, ensuring that passionate students, especially in Nepal, can pursue their educational aspirations in technology and related fields.”
  • Tuition, exam fees, housing, and technology grants
  • Mentoring from Nepali engineers and global volunteers
  • Digital literacy for students and their families

What drives us

Education is a proven path to economic stability in Nepal. When a student stays in school, siblings follow, local businesses grow, and communities gain tech-ready leaders.

Our story

From Jyamrung to global technology hubs.

I grew up in Jyamrung, Dhading, where electricity showed up when I was already in grade eight. Evenings meant homework under candlelight and nine-kilometer walks on goat trails to reach a classroom with no computers. Those candles felt like steady little promises that tomorrow could be different.

I first touched a computer in Kathmandu. The glow of that monitor felt like a door being unlocked. Aashirbad carries my father’s name and his belief that education is the greatest gift. His blessing pushed me through Texas Tech University for a BS in Computer Science and an MS in Software Engineering, and today I serve as a Senior Data Engineer at Veeva Systems.

The hills still stay with me. I remember friends wrapping notebooks in plastic each monsoon, families sharing a single winter coat, and girls missing class because there was no hostel in town. Aashirbad Foundation exists because talent is universal, opportunity is not. Every scholarship is a candle we pass forward to students climbing the same paths I climbed, and diaspora mentors help keep that candle lit.
