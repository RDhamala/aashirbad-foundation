The Problem

Talent is everywhere. Access is not.

In rural communities like Dhading, students face financial barriers that can end a school year early, limited access to mentors and professional networks, and little exposure to technology or modern learning tools. None of that reflects a student's ability — it reflects where they happened to grow up.

What we do

Three simple programs

Education access, mentorship, and digital access keep students enrolled, guided, and connected to opportunity.

Why We Started

Potential is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

Aashirbad Foundation grew out of firsthand experience: our founder grew up in a remote village in Nepal without electricity, internet, motor roads, or computers until high school, and later became a first-generation professional working in technology. That experience shaped a simple belief — talented students in remote communities deserve earlier exposure to technology, guidance, and opportunities beyond their immediate surroundings.
