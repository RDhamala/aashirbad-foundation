Your gift can change a life.

Your tax-deductible donation funds scholarships, technology, and mentorship for students in rural Nepal. Choose a one-time gift, monthly support, device sponsorship, or a full scholarship - every option keeps a student in class.

Every gift makes a difference

We use 100% of donations to support students directly and publish annual reports for transparency.

  • $35 covers exam and lab fees for a student this term.
  • $75 keeps a student online for a month with weekly mentor check-ins.
  • $350 outfits a student with a rugged laptop, case, and onboarding.
  • $1,200 sponsors a year of tuition, housing, exams, and mentoring for a scholar.

What each option covers

  • $35 Exam and lab fees for a term

Device sponsorship
