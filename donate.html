Give online

Give securely through PayPal. You'll confirm your amount in the PayPal window. General gifts go where the need is greatest: helping a student stay in school, connect with a mentor, or gain access to technology.

Aashirbad Foundation is a registered U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN: 93-4943678). Donations are tax-deductible. We are volunteer-led with no paid staff and keep admin costs minimal, so your gift goes toward student support. We publish annual reports for transparency.

What your gift can do

  • $35 covers exam and lab fees for a student for a term.
  • $75 keeps a student connected for a month of weekly mentor check-ins.
  • $350 helps expand shared computers and digital learning at a partner school.
  • $1,200 sponsors a full year of tuition, hostel, exams, and mentoring for one scholar.

Other ways to give

Want to give monthly, sponsor a scholarship, or give by check, ACH, donor-advised fund, stock, or employer match? Tell us what you have in mind and we'll send instructions.

How often?

This is a pledge, not a payment. We'll follow up to arrange your gift securely.

We never share or sell your information. Your details are used only to arrange your gift and send your tax receipt.

Directing your gift

General gifts go where the need is greatest. If you'd like your gift to support something specific, mention it in the pledge form or contact us.

Digital Opportunity

Helps provide shared computers, digital learning resources, and technology exposure at a partner school, with setup and maintenance support.

Scholarship sponsorship

$1,200 a year covers tuition, hostel, exam and lab fees, and weekly mentor check-ins, with quarterly progress updates.

What you get back

  • A tax receipt within 48 hours of your gift.
  • A term-end email showing which students your gift supported.
  • Our annual report each January with full financials.

Partner with us
