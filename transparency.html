How funds move

From donation to student.

  1. You donate via PayPal, Venmo, or by mail.
  2. The board reviews and approves each disbursement.
  3. Funds are transferred to our program lead in Nepal.
  4. Goma coordinates with headteachers to pay fees, purchase devices, or fund mentor stipends.
  5. Donors receive a receipt and a term-end update showing where funds went.

What we can share today

  • IRS determination letter — available on request
  • Form 990 — filed annually, available on request
  • Donor receipts — sent after every gift
  • Term-end updates — emailed to donors each semester

An honest note

What we’re still building.

We are a small, volunteer-run organization founded in 2023. We do not yet have audited financial statements or multi-year outcome data. The oldest cohort of scholars is still in school. As results come in, we will publish them here and in our annual reports. If you have questions about anything—our finances, our programs, our plans—please reach out directly. We would rather answer a hard question than leave you guessing.
