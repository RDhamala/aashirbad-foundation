How funds move

Bridge in three steps.

  1. U.S.-based donors contribute to Aashirbad Foundation (EIN 93-4943678).
  2. Compliance and review: board finance committee approves disbursements and converts funds through regulated channels.
  3. Nepali delivery partners such as community schools, Teach For Nepal alumni, and Girls in Tech Nepal hubs deploy scholarships, devices, and labs with quarterly reporting.

What donors receive

  • Quarterly dashboards showing spending, beneficiaries, and stories.
  • Annual IRS filings, audited statements, and partner MOUs.
  • Direct contact with our Fort Worth compliance lead for any verification.

Policies

Governance & accountability.

  • Gift acceptance policy: ensures funds align with mission.
  • Conflict of interest policy: reviewed annually by board.
  • Data privacy policy: protects donor and scholar information.

Board oversight

Our board finance committee reviews budgets quarterly and oversees independent audits.
