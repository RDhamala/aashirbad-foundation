Our Funding Model

From your heart to their hands.

Your donation, whether made online or by mail, is received by Aashirbad Foundation, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in the United States. Our volunteer board of directors reviews and approves all disbursements, ensuring that every dollar is used effectively. Funds are then transferred to our trusted partners in Nepal, who work directly with our students and communities to provide scholarships, laptops, and other support. We are committed to ensuring that your donation has the greatest possible impact.

Our Commitment to Good Governance

The rules that keep us accountable.

We are committed to the highest standards of governance and accountability. Our policies are designed to ensure that we are always acting in the best interests of our students, our donors, and the communities we serve.

  • Gift Acceptance: To ensure that all donations are used in a manner that is consistent with our mission and values.
  • Conflict of Interest: To ensure that all decisions are made in the best interests of our students and our organization.
  • Data Privacy: To protect the privacy and confidentiality of our students, donors, and partners.
