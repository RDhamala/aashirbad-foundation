2024 snapshot

Key outcomes across Nepal.

92% graduation success

Scholarship recipients completed their current academic year with GPA targets met and renewed funding.

1,200+ devices deployed

Students received laptops or tablets plus training to participate in remote STEM courses.

48 mentors active

Volunteer mentors logged 4,500 coaching hours with scholars preparing for internships.

65% first-generation

Most scholars are the first in their family to pursue higher education, multiplying community impact.
