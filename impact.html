Impact

Results you can measure and trust.

We share data, student stories, and third-party audits so supporters understand how their gifts translate into opportunity.

2024 snapshot

Key outcomes across Nepal.

Donations from the United States are tracked in real time so we can show exactly how many scholars receive hostel stipends, how many Wi-Fi nodes reach mountain classrooms, and how many women-led labs stay open during winter load-shedding.

92% graduation success

Scholarship recipients completed their current academic year with GPA targets met and renewed funding.

1,200+ devices deployed

Students received laptops or tablets plus training to participate in remote STEM courses.

48 mentors active

Volunteer mentors logged 4,500 coaching hours with scholars preparing for internships.

65% first-generation

Most scholars are the first in their family to pursue higher education, multiplying community impact.

Evidence-based

Tracking what matters.

We collect data on enrollment, graduation, employment, and community projects—plus basic well-being indicators such as device uptime, winter clothing distribution, and hostel safety. The information flows into dashboards shared with donors each quarter and mirrors indicators used by Teach For Nepal, Karkhana Samuha, and the Government’s Digital Nepal Framework.

  • Scholar surveys measuring confidence and belonging
  • Internship placement rates in Nepal and abroad
  • Community service hours contributed by alumni
