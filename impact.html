Our Accomplishments

A look at what we’ve achieved together.

We began piloting in 2023, starting in Dhading. Here are the results so far, with annual updates to keep donors informed as we scale a low-cost model to similar rural communities.

100+ Students Supported

Scholarships keep students in class with tuition, housing, exams, and books covered so finances don’t interrupt their studies.

10+ Devices Placed

Laptops and tablets delivered to rural schools with onboarding, so students can access lessons, mentors, and assignments.

95% Retention

Scholars remain enrolled year over year with regular mentor check-ins and timely fee coverage.

40+ Mentors

Nepali mentors volunteer weekly for coaching, study support, and career prep, keeping first-generation students on track.

Our Approach to Impact

Measuring what matters most.

We track academic performance, retention rates, and mentor engagement to understand what’s working and where we can improve. This data shapes every decision we make about our programs.

  • Listening to our students’ voices through regular surveys and feedback sessions.
  • Tracking our students’ progress from their first day of college to their first day on the job.
  • Working with our alumni to measure their long-term impact on their communities.
