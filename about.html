Our Story

From a small village in Nepal to a global community of support.

Our founder, Roshan Dhamala, walked an hour to school every day in a remote Nepali village. Without electricity, computers, or internet access until middle school, he experienced firsthand the barriers rural students face. Aashirbad Foundation is his way of ensuring today’s students have the resources he lacked.

Our Commitment

We are in this together.

We partner with local leaders, teachers, and families to identify students who need support. Our programs provide holistic assistance—from financial aid to mentorship—ensuring every student has the tools to succeed.

  • Community-led selection: Local committees help ensure support reaches the students who need it most.
  • Mentorship: Mentors from similar backgrounds provide personal guidance and career support.
  • Regular check-ins: We stay connected with students to ensure they are thriving in and out of the classroom.

Our Promise

Aashirbad Foundation is a registered U.S. 501(c)(3) (EIN: 93-4943678) headquartered in the United States, with a dedicated team on the ground in Nepal. We pilot in Dhading today, and we are building a low-cost model that can scale to similar rural communities in the future.

We follow U.S. governance, financial controls, and safeguarding standards for all programs, regardless of location, and we publish receipts and impact updates for donors.

Our Story

Born from a teacher’s belief in education.

Aashirbad Foundation began in Jyamrung, Dhading, where our founder studied by candlelight, walked steep hills to school, and first saw a computer only after moving to Kathmandu. He later earned a BS in Computer Science and an MS in Software Engineering from Texas Tech University and now works as a Senior Data Engineer at Veeva Systems. The foundation is named for his late father—a high-school teacher who believed education is the greatest gift. Guided by that legacy, we pilot in Dhading and design a model that can scale to similar rural communities.

The Need

The Reality for Many Children in Nepal

In rural Nepal, poverty forces many families to choose between sending their children to school and meeting basic needs. Some children walk hours through difficult terrain to reach class. Those who do arrive often lack books, supplies, and qualified teachers.

  • Lack of access to quality education and qualified teachers.
  • Inadequate school supplies and warm clothing.
  • Long and dangerous journeys to school, especially for children in remote areas.
  • The ongoing impact of the 2015 earthquake, which destroyed thousands of schools.

Our Process

Listening to the needs of the community.

Our process is simple and community-driven. We listen to the needs of the community and work with local leaders to identify students who are at risk of leaving school. We then provide them with the support they need to continue their education and pursue their dreams.

  • Community-based nominations: Teachers, principals, and community leaders nominate students who are at risk of dropping out.
  • Personalized support: Our team meets with each student to understand their unique needs and create a personalized support plan.
  • Direct impact: Your donations go directly to supporting our students, with regular updates on their progress.

The Power of Your Support

In many parts of Nepal, families are forced to make impossible choices between education and other basic needs. Your support can help us break this cycle of poverty and empower the next generation of leaders in Nepal.

Our Values

The principles that guide our work

Our values are at the heart of everything we do. They reflect our commitment to the students and communities we serve, and they guide our decisions every day.
