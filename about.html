Our Story

Named in memory of a teacher who believed in education.

Aashirbad Foundation was founded by Roshan Dhamala, who grew up in a remote village in Jyamrung, Dhading, without electricity, internet, motor roads, or access to a computer until high school. The foundation is named in memory of his late father, a village teacher who believed education was the greatest gift a child could receive.

Our Programs Our Team

Mission

To expand access to education, mentorship, and technology for young people in underserved communities in Nepal.

Why Aashirbad Exists

A blessing passed forward.

Aashirbad means "blessing." The foundation is named in memory of Roshan Dhamala's father, a schoolteacher who believed deeply in education. Roshan grew up in a remote village in Jyamrung, Dhading, without reliable electricity, internet, motor roads, or access to a computer until high school. He later earned a BS in Computer Science and an MS in Software Engineering from Texas Tech University, and built a career in data and technology. Growing up without those things stayed with him.

That experience shaped a simple belief: young people with talent deserve access to education, guidance, and technology earlier in life. In 2023, he started Aashirbad Foundation to build that support in Dhading, the district where he grew up.

Why Dhading?

In rural Nepal, families often face financial barriers, limited access to mentors and professional networks, and limited exposure to technology or modern learning tools. Dhading is where our founder grew up and where we have the relationships to make programs work.

Operating Model

Community-led, donor-verified.

We don’t decide who gets support from a desk in Texas. Headteachers and ward committees in Dhading identify students who need support; our Nepal-based team verifies each case, and our U.S. board oversees governance and financial controls. Donors receive updates each term.

  • Headteachers and local leaders identify students who need support.
  • Our Nepal team meets with each student and their family to verify need.
  • Our U.S. board reviews and approves disbursements.
  • Donors receive a term-end update showing where funds went.
