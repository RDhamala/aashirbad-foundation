Who we are

Centered on equity, anchored in Nepal.

Our mission is to empower aspiring minds by providing transformative scholarships. We are dedicated to breaking financial barriers and fostering inclusivity, ensuring that passionate students, especially in Nepal, can pursue their educational aspirations in technology and related fields.

Approach

Scholar-centered support at every step.

We co-design programs with headteachers, municipal education units, Teach For Nepal alumni, and Women Leaders in Technology mentors. Together we pick scholars, map out laptop + hostel needs, and review academic data each quarter so no one falls behind.

  • Ward selection panels include community school heads and women representatives.
  • Diaspora engineers and local alumni mentor every scholar through code reviews and career checkpoints.
  • Quarterly wellness visits verify stipends, hostel receipts, and device upkeep.

Operating model

Aashirbad Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) headquartered in Fort Worth, TX with on-the-ground coordinators in Nepal. We distribute funds through vetted partners and alumni-led cooperatives to ensure compliance, speed, and local ownership.

How it works

Community-led identification.

Aashirbad Foundation partners directly with public schools, rural learning centers, and ward education committees across Nepal. Teachers, principals, and community leaders submit cases when students are about to leave school because they cannot afford tuition, food, winter clothing, or travel to urban campuses.

  • Ward committees and headteachers flag students at risk of dropping out.
  • Coordinators conduct field visits to document exact costs and needs.
  • U.S. donors sponsor verified cases, and partners report quarterly.

Why this matters

Families in Karnali, Dolakha, and the Terai often sacrifice meals, coats, or exam fees for a single child. Keeping decision-making in local hands ensures support reaches the right students while giving donors confidence that every rupee is accounted for.

Values

Guiding principles

We anchor every decision around student well-being, data-driven learning, and transparency with donors.
