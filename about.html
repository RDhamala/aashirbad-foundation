Who we are

Centered on equity, anchored in Nepal.

Our mission is to empower aspiring minds by providing transformative scholarships. We are dedicated to breaking financial barriers and fostering inclusivity, ensuring that passionate students, especially in Nepal, can pursue their educational aspirations in technology and related fields.

Approach

Scholar-centered support at every step.

We co-design programs with educators and local leaders to select scholars, map individualized learning plans, and track progress through graduation. Wraparound support covers tuition, technology, housing, transportation, and career mentoring.

  • Community-based selection panels
  • Mentors paired with each scholar
  • Quarterly wellness and academic check-ins

Operating model

Aashirbad Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) headquartered in Seattle, WA with on-the-ground coordinators in Nepal. We distribute funds through vetted partners and alumni-led cooperatives to ensure compliance, speed, and local ownership.

Values

Guiding principles

We anchor every decision around student well-being, data-driven learning, and transparency with donors.

  • Equity: prioritize students historically excluded from STEM
  • Community trust: parents, teachers, and alumni guide investments
  • Impact: measure outcomes beyond graduation

Partners

We collaborate with schools in Kathmandu, Pokhara, Dolakha, and the Terai region plus diaspora-led tech collectives.
