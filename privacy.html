Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Subscribe to our newsletter (email address)
  • Submit a contact form (name, email, message)
  • Make a donation through PayPal (name, email, donation amount)

We do not collect information automatically through cookies, trackers, or analytics tools. This is a static website with no server-side data collection.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send newsletter updates about our programs and impact
  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Process and acknowledge donations
  • Provide tax receipts for charitable contributions

How we protect your information

Forms are processed through Formspree, a secure third-party form service. Donations are processed through PayPal's secure payment platform. We do not store credit card numbers or payment details on our website.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to process donations (PayPal) and form submissions (Formspree), or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request to view, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at info@aashirbadfoundation.org. To unsubscribe from our newsletter, reply to any email with "unsubscribe" or contact us directly.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
